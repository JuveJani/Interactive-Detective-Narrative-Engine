# Offline AI Workflow

## When to use local AI

Use on-device AI when you want a second opinion on a **single finding** without uploading the whole repository.

Human-readable reports (`executive_diagnostic.md`, `explanations/`) are enough without AI.

## Workflow

1. Run `./run_full_diagnostic.sh` or `python3 idne_sim.py simulate ...`
2. Export context: `./export_latest_for_ai.sh SIM-FAKE-J-122`
3. Open `OFFLINE_AI_SYSTEM_PROMPT.md` as the system prompt in your local AI app
4. Paste `OFFLINE_AI_USER_PROMPT_TEMPLATE.md` with the finding ID filled in
5. Attach or paste `local_ai_context/finding_context_<ID>.md`
6. Review the AI response against **proven facts** in the context file
7. Approve or reject suggested edits manually

## Files the AI receives

Each `finding_context_<ID>.json` / `.md` includes only:

- Relevant engine rule snippets (not the full engine doc)
- Node excerpt for the cited identifier
- Simulator evidence and metrics snapshot
- Repair options already generated by the repair advisor
- Validation commands
- Explicit "do not invent" constraints

## Safety

- The simulator never modifies your repository by default.
- `proposed_fix_*.patch` files are placeholders until a human writes a real diff.
- Do not let the AI change player story without playtest.
