# The Last Witness — Prototype Brief

## Prototype Status

Planned as the first complete IDNE validation adventure.

## Target Experience

- approximately two hours;
- playable solo or cooperatively by two players;
- detective investigation with fair evidence;
- limited bookkeeping;
- meaningful time pressure;
- private information during selected two-player scenes;
- multiple endings based on deduction and action, not arbitrary guessing.

## Engine Features Under Test

- authoritative world time;
- public and private knowledge separation;
- compile-time event variant resolution;
- clue redundancy;
- off-screen NPC activity;
- split-scene synchronization;
- terminal node classification;
- authored fallback events;
- controlled replayability where practical.

## Content Budget

- 2 investigators;
- 1 central case;
- 3 to 5 major suspects;
- 4 to 7 locations;
- 12 to 20 meaningful clues;
- no more than 3 split-scene windows;
- 3 to 6 terminal outcomes.

## Production Sequence

1. Define the case truth and causal timeline.
2. Define suspects, motives, secrets, and knowledge states.
3. Build the clue dependency map.
4. Define locations and time costs.
5. Define off-screen NPC events.
6. Build solo and two-player adventure logic.
7. Compile public nodes and private player outputs.
8. Validate links, timing, clue access, and endings.
9. Run the first playtest.
10. Classify defects and update the engine only where evidence justifies it.
