- playtest questionnaire.

## Scope controls

The two-hour prototype should use approximately:

- 8 active investigation locations;
- 10 significant NPCs, but no more than 6 requiring full interviews;
- 4 major time blocks;
- 3 planned split opportunities;
- 1 forced reunion before the final act;
- 6-8 ending variants compiled from the framework.

Do not add new principal conspirators unless playtesting reveals a specific need.

## First internal review questions

1. Is the voluntary disappearance fair to infer?
2. Is Rook's corruption discoverable without one mandatory clue?
3. Is Lena suspicious without becoming obviously innocent?
4. Can players reach Signal Room 4B through at least three routes?
5. Can Elias be saved after one major investigative mistake?
6. Can a failed roll create cost rather than a dead end?
7. Does each player possess unique useful information?
8. Can the final act be completed without one player becoming a spectator?
