# DO NOT READ: Case Overview

## Case identity

**Case title:** The Last Witness  
**Primary location:** Greyhaven, a declining coastal city  
**Playable investigation window:** Friday 18:30 to Saturday 02:00  
**Narrative deadline:** a scheduled evidence transfer at 02:00  
**Apparent crime:** kidnapping of protected witness Elias Varga  
**Actual situation:** voluntary disappearance followed by coercion, concealment, and one attempted murder  
**Central question:** Can the investigators identify whom Elias was trying to expose, find him alive, and decide whom to trust before the evidence disappears?

## The truth in one page

Elias Varga was the financial controller of **Northstar Renewal**, a public-private redevelopment program. He discovered that emergency-housing funds were being redirected through shell contractors. The scheme was created by Deputy Mayor **Mara Vale** and implemented by contractor **Jonas Krell**. Police inspector **Adrian Rook** protected the operation by burying complaints and intimidating witnesses.

Elias agreed to testify. He did not trust the official witness-protection plan because Rook controlled the transport team. With help from investigative journalist **Nadia Soren**, Elias staged his own disappearance. He left his apartment through a service passage at 17:42 and planned to hide in an unused signal room beneath the old ferry terminal until Nadia could deliver his encrypted ledger to an external prosecutor at 02:00.

The plan failed for three independent reasons:

1. Elias secretly contacted his estranged sister **Lena Varga**, who followed him.
2. Nadia's editor, **Marcus Hale**, sold partial information about the meeting to Jonas Krell to save the newspaper from bankruptcy.
3. Rook intercepted a police database search made by the players' future contact, patrol officer **Mina Cho**, and began tracing Elias through the city's camera network.

At 19:06, Lena found Elias at the ferry terminal. They argued. She wanted him to surrender the ledger and flee the country. Elias refused. At 19:18, Krell's fixer **Silas Reed** entered the terminal, intending to seize the ledger without killing Elias. Lena struck Reed with a steel flashlight. During the struggle, Elias fell down a maintenance stair and suffered a severe head injury.

Lena and Reed both believed Elias might die. Reed fled with Elias's decoy phone. Lena hid Elias inside the sealed signal room and called Dr. **Iris Bell**, a former emergency physician whose medical license had been suspended after she falsified records to protect an abused patient. Iris treated Elias in secret because calling an ambulance would expose him to Rook.

Elias remains alive but deteriorating. He has an intracranial bleed and requires surgery before approximately 01:15. Iris knows this but delays because Elias, intermittently conscious, insists that the evidence transfer must occur first.

No one initially knows the complete situation:

- Nadia thinks Elias reached the safe room alone and later went silent.
- Rook thinks Krell captured him.
- Krell thinks Lena has either killed or abducted him.
- Lena believes Nadia betrayed Elias.
- Iris believes every official institution is compromised.
- Mina Cho suspects Rook but has no proof.
- Marcus Hale is trying to erase evidence of his leak.

The players can discover Elias, save him, expose the conspiracy, expose only part of it, protect him while losing the case, or misidentify the threat and deliver him to Rook.

## Principal antagonism

There is no single simple villain in the immediate disappearance.

- **Mara Vale** designed the corruption scheme and remains physically absent for most of the adventure.
- **Adrian Rook** is the most dangerous active antagonist. He uses legitimate police authority to seize evidence and control the investigation.
- **Jonas Krell** ordered the recovery of the ledger and accepts violence, but he did not order Elias's death.
- **Silas Reed** caused the physical struggle but did not push Elias deliberately.
- **Marcus Hale** betrayed Nadia and Elias for financial survival, then attempts to conceal it.
- **Lena Varga** hides an injured man and obstructs help, but does so from fear and loyalty.
- **Iris Bell** provides necessary medical aid while dangerously delaying definitive treatment.

## Fair solution

A strong solution requires the players to combine at least three independent chains:

### Chain A: Elias disappeared voluntarily

- apartment service-door trace;
- packed medication and missing travel documents;
- deleted message fragment to Nadia;
- decoy signs of forced entry created after Elias left.

### Chain B: the ferry terminal is the real destination

- tide-chart notation in Elias's notebook;
- Nadia's old photograph containing the signal-room number;
- transit-card use near Pier 6;
- maintenance map found at the municipal archive or terminal office.

### Chain C: official police protection is compromised

- Rook's unauthorized camera request;
- Mina Cho's altered incident report;
- call records linking Rook to Krell;
- evidence that the supposed rescue order was created after Rook learned the players were close.

### Chain D: Elias is alive but critically injured

- blood pattern inconsistent with fatal blood loss;
- Iris's missing trauma supplies;
- Lena's contradictory travel timeline;
- heat or sound from the sealed signal room.

No single clue reveals the entire solution.

## Intended emotional core

The case is not primarily about identifying a theatrical murderer. It is about deciding what truth can survive when every institution and every witness is compromised.

The final moral tension is:

- expose everything immediately and risk Elias's life;
- save Elias first and risk losing the evidence;
- trust an imperfect ally;
- or attempt to control both rescue and exposure with limited time.
