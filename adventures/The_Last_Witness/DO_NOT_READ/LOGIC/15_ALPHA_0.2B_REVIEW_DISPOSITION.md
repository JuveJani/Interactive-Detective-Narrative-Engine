# DO NOT READ: Alpha 0.2b Review Disposition

## Review-handling rule

External findings are applied only when they correspond to entities, locations, timelines, and mechanics actually present in the canonical adventure package.

Generic or mismatched examples are not imported into the case merely because they appear in a review. The canonical World Bible remains authoritative.

## Applied review categories

The following useful audit principles are now explicitly implemented:

- every physical item transition requires a state change or transit event;
- NPC knowledge requires a traceable ingestion path;
- travel costs and NPC movement use the same clock model;
- high-risk disclosure requires evidence or leverage;
- critical deductions have redundant routes;
- lost access items do not create permanent soft-locks;
- failed checks transform cost or certainty rather than deleting progress;
- accusations are proof-gated;
- wrong accusations receive target-specific resolution;
- split-party tasks remain locally solvable;
- final-act responsibilities preserve player parity;
- entity keys and location variants are explicit.

## Not imported

Any review example referring to non-canonical entities, murder weapons, toxicology, laboratories, manors, bank vaults, forty-eight-hour schedules, or other absent story elements is treated as illustrative reviewer boilerplate rather than a factual finding about `The Last Witness`.

## Readiness result

The adventure is ready for:

- detailed node compilation;
- player-specific scene allocation;
- narrative drafting;
- first printable alpha construction.

It is not yet ready for public play because the player books and synchronized narrative passages have not been compiled.
