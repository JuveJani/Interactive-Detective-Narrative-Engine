# DO NOT READ: Pre-Logic Audit Resolution

## Purpose

This file records the categories resolved before detailed Adventure Logic. It deliberately avoids copying external review language and treats the canonical case documents as source of truth.

## Resolved now

### Physical state integrity

- Added immutable item keys.
- Added explicit transit records for the primary ledger, decoy ledger, photograph, medical kit, phone, and reports.
- Added one-location-at-a-time state rule.

### NPC knowledge integrity

- Defined initial knowledge and explicit update paths.
- Prevented dialogue from leaking objective truth.
- Replaced easy single-roll confessions with staged disclosure and hard leverage.

### Timeline realism

- Standardized realistic city travel and action costs.
- Applied the same movement rules to NPCs and players.
- Added storm and emergency-response modifiers.

### Fair-play redundancy

- Major conclusions now have multiple independent clue routes.
- Reed's presence, Rook's corruption, room identification, and terminal access do not depend on one locked object or one successful check.

### Soft-lock prevention

- Added at least two legal terminal access routes in every state.
- Added alternate room-identification sources.
- Added multiple trusted rescue vectors.
- Converted lost evidence into weaker outcomes rather than endless loops.

### Ending validation

- Accusations are evidence-gated.
- Wrong accusations receive target-specific counter-resolution.
- Rescue, truth, and public proof are scored separately.

### Two-player operation

- Split branches remain independently actionable.
- Critical live puzzles are deferred until regroup.
- Final act assigns parallel responsibilities.

## Deferred to playtesting

- exact frequency of player communication;
- final balance between rescue and evidence transfer;
- emotional impact and clarity of specific ending prose.
