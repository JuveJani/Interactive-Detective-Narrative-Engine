# DO NOT READ: Location Database

## Location-state principles

Every location has:

- a base physical state;
- time-dependent changes;
- access requirements;
- available clue classes;
- risks;
- possible off-screen changes.

Locations should not exist only as containers for one clue.

---

## LOC-01: Elias Varga's Apartment

### Function

Establish that the apparent kidnapping was staged and introduce police interference.

### Physical layout

- small entrance hall;
- kitchen/living room;
- bedroom;
- bathroom;
- balcony;
- shared laundry room;
- rear service corridor.

### Initial state at 20:05

Police tape is present. Mina Cho can provide limited access. Rook's detectives have already photographed the scene but have not found the service corridor.

### Objective clues

1. **Blood droplets**
   - small volume;
   - chemically preserved;
   - inconsistent with active injury.

2. **Broken phone**
   - no skin or blood on impact surface;
   - crack pattern consistent with placement and later crushing.

3. **Missing medication**
   - Elias requires daily medication.
   - empty travel case is present.

4. **Passport concealment**
   - hidden envelope is empty.
   - dust outline proves something was removed.

5. **Service latch**
   - disturbed from inside.
   - damp fibre matching Elias's dark coat.

6. **Timed crash device**
   - may be found behind a cabinet after careful search.

### False lead

A neighbour reports a “hooded abductor.” The person was Elias himself.

### Time changes

- 21:00: Rook limits access.
- 22:15: a loyal detective removes the timed device if it has not been found.
- after 23:00: apartment becomes inaccessible without illegal entry or Mina's help.

## LOC-08: Signal Room 4B

### Function

Hidden rescue location, evidence cache, and final moral decision point.

### Contents

- Elias on improvised bedding;
- Iris's medical equipment;
- Lena;
- primary hardware key;
- old signal battery;
- ferry windows photograph;
- emergency radio;
- maintenance exit.

### Initial state when found

Depends on time:

- before 23:00: Elias intermittently conscious;
- 23:00-00:30: confused, vomiting, declining;
- 00:30-01:00: mostly unconscious;
- after 01:00: unresponsive.

### Final pressures

Players may need to choose sequencing among:

- ambulance request;
- secure evidence copy;
- trust Mina;
- confront Lena;
- stop Reed;
- prevent Rook from taking control;
- complete transfer.

### Information isolation

If players arrive separately, the first player may enter while the second remains outside dealing with another threat. The final player books should support this without allowing either player to read the other's private scene.

## LOC-10: St. Orison Medical Centre

### Function

Ending destination rather than primary investigation location.

### Constraint

Rook can influence normal police intake but does not control the entire hospital. A safe transfer requires either:

- Mina preserving the chain of custody;
- public exposure of Rook;
- direct contact with an independent emergency physician;
- or a diversion that prevents Rook intercepting Elias.

### Ending impact

Arrival before 01:15 gives the highest survival probability. Later arrival may still succeed but with severe consequences.
