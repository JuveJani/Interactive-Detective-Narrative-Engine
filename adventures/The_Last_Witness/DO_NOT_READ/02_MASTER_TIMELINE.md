# DO NOT READ: Master Timeline

## Timeline conventions

- Times are local Greyhaven time.
- “Fixed” events occur regardless of player action unless explicitly marked as interruptible.
- “Conditional” events depend on player choices or discovery.
- The playable opening is **Friday 20:00**.
- The external evidence-transfer deadline is **Saturday 02:00**.
