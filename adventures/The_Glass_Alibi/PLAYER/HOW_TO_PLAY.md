# How to Play

## Goal

You are contract investigators at the Helix Meridian campus. A senior automation architect died during an after-hours test. Corporate leadership wants the incident classified tonight. You must investigate, compare findings, and decide what to do before the official report locks at **00:30**.

## Cooperation

- Read **joint scenes** together aloud.
- During **split scenes**, only the assigned player reads their private booklet.
- When a split scene ends, **wait** for your partner unless you use a legal communication action (below).
- At **regroup** scenes, share discoveries and update the shared case file.

## Time

- The case uses a **shared clock** starting at **19:00** Saturday.
- Each scene lists a **time cost**. Add it to the clock when you finish that scene.
- Soft reminders appear in joint scenes (for example, regroup windows near **20:30** and **22:45**). You are never forced to skip content by the clock alone.
- The narrative deadline is **00:30** Sunday.

## Skill checks

When a scene calls for a check:

```text
roll = d20 + your modifier for that skill
success if roll ≥ the listed DC
```

- If both players may attempt, **each rolls**; use the **highest successful** result.
- **Pass:** read the success outcome and record any clue listed.
- **Fail:** read the failure outcome; alternate routes may still exist later.

Difficulty bands used in this adventure:

| Band | DC |
|---|---:|
| Medium | 10 |
| Hard | 15 |

Modifiers are on your character sheet.

## Clues

- When you earn a clue, record its **code** (for example `C-03`) and short title on the shared clue log.
- Private clues stay with you until regroup; then decide what to share.
- Clues are **not** consumed when shared.

## Communication during splits

| Mode | Cost | Rule |
|---|---:|---|
| **Wait** | 0 min | Default when your branch is finished |
| **Phone call** | 5 min | One concise fact or decision; not inside RF-shielded test bay |
| **Physical regroup** | 10 min | Full sharing; use only when a joint scene permits early regroup |
| **Campus message** | 0–10 min | If a scene allows delayed text; no live puzzle solving |

## Decisions

- Joint scenes may offer numbered choices. Agree as a pair unless the text says otherwise.
- If you disagree on a joint action, each states your priority; the scene tells you how to resolve conflict.
- At the formal accusation scene, you must agree on a single named target.

## Ending

- Complete accusation, evidence preservation, and custody scenes as directed.
- Open `ENDINGS.md` only from **J-900**.
- Follow the resolver questions honestly based on your case file.

## Fair play

- Use only information your investigators discovered in play.
- Do not look up scene codes early.
- Do not consult `DO_NOT_READ/` or online spoilers.
