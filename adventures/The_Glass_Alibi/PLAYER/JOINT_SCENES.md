## J-100 — Incident Briefing

**Time cost:** +10 minutes → clock should read **19:10**  
**Location:** Helix Meridian briefing point, South Wing lobby

Rain needles the glass wall of the security lobby. Temporary tables hold sealed evidence bags, a campus map, and a single-page incident summary stamped **PRELIMINARY — NOT FOR RELEASE**.

A corporate counsel liaison you've only met by phone reminds you that county notification is scheduled for morning, but **tonight's internal report locks at 00:30**. Whatever you find has to be documented before then.

### What you are told

- **Dr. Elena Park**, Chief Automation Architect, was found unresponsive in **Automated Test Bay 3** after a CO₂ fire-suppression purge.
- Initial campus assessment: tragic **PLC fault** during authorized solo firmware validation.
- The body remains in the bay pending your imaging. Medical has confirmed death.
- **Dana Cole**, CFO liaison embedded at Helix, is on site and "fully cooperative."
- **Kevin Marsh** is holding the SCADA historian in the monitoring room.
- **Sable Ortiz** staffs the security desk ten meters away.
- Five personnel are queued for credential review: **Marcus Hale** (operations), **Dr. Priya Nair** (architect), **Vince Calder** (security contractor), **Tom Reyes** (maintenance), and Dana.

You have read-only controller access and escorted bay entry on your credentials. Nobody on campus knows you yet; use that distance.

### Atmosphere

Fluorescents hum. The HelixSense dashboard behind Sable shows a green **ALL CLEAR** that feels too calm. Dana offers coffee and a quiet word: Elena insisted on working alone to meet Monday's audit deadline. The statement is delivered like weather — factual, not debatable.
