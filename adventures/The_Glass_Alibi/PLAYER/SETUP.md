# Setup Instructions

## What you need

| Item | Quantity | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| Investigators | 2 | No facilitator required |
| d20 die | 1 | Shared or one per player |
| Pencils | 2 | For case file and clue log |
| Printed booklets **or** two devices | — | See printing below |
| Timer (optional) | 1 | Real-time limit not required |

## Printing and device layout

### Recommended (print)

Print and staple **separately**:

1. `SHARED/CASE_FILE.md` — **one copy, shared**
2. `CHARACTERS/CHARACTER_SHEET_SYSTEMS.md` — one per systems-role player
3. `CHARACTERS/CHARACTER_SHEET_FIELD.md` — one per field-role player
4. `JOINT_SCENES.md` — **one copy, shared**
5. `BOOKLET_SYSTEMS.md` — **systems-role player only** (seal or clip closed)
6. `BOOKLET_FIELD.md` — **field-role player only** (seal or clip closed)
7. `ENDINGS.md` — **one copy, shared** (keep closed until `J-900`)

Optional: print `HOW_TO_PLAY.md` once for reference.

### Digital play

- Use **three tabs minimum**: Shared (joint + case file), Systems booklet, Field booklet.
- Only the player assigned to a private booklet may view that file.
- Hide or do not bookmark `ENDINGS.md` until directed.

## Role assignment

Choose who plays which emphasis. Either assignment is valid.

| Role | Booklet | Character sheet | Typical strengths |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Systems investigator** | `BOOKLET_SYSTEMS.md` | `CHARACTER_SHEET_SYSTEMS.md` | controllers, logs, metadata, finance terminals |
| **Field investigator** | `BOOKLET_FIELD.md` | `CHARACTER_SHEET_FIELD.md` | physical scenes, perimeter, interviews, access |

You may swap roles at the briefing if both agree.

## Spoiler safety

- Do not read private booklets before the joint scene sends you there.
- Do not read past your current scene code.
- At regroup, share only what your character would say aloud; use the clue log in `SHARED/CASE_FILE.md`.
- `ENDINGS.md` is locked until **J-900**.

## Starting the game

1. Write **19:00** on the case file clock.
2. Open `JOINT_SCENES.md` at **J-100** together.
3. Follow navigation instructions on each page.
