# The Glass Alibi — Prototype Brief

## Prototype Status

First complete IDNE validation adventure design package for a modern two-player detective mystery.

## Target Experience

- approximately two hours;
- cooperative two-player investigation (**Alpha 0.2c logic:** `two_player` only);
- fair-play evidence with multiple independent routes;
- limited bookkeeping on a shared world clock;
- realistic tone; no supernatural elements;
- private information during split scenes;
- multiple endings driven by deduction and action.

## Engine Features Under Test

- authoritative shared world clock;
- D20 check resolution with character-sheet modifiers;
- scene mode metadata (Joint / Split);
- split-and-regroup flow with participation audit;
- clue redundancy and soft-lock prevention;
- terminal ending dispatch;
- wrong-accusation handling.
