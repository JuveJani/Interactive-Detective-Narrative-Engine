# Character sheet — County occupational safety investigator

You investigate workplace fatalities and verify employer incident reports.

## Check modifiers

| Capability | Modifier | When it applies |
|---|---|---|
| **Perception** | **+2** | Physical inspection and environmental observation checks |
| **Reasoning** | **+2** | Timeline and physical evidence reconstruction checks |
| **Technical** | **+1** | Equipment and hydraulic system checks |

County tablet, measuring tape, evidence camera, pocket field guide.

File a supported preliminary report before the 1200 deadline.
