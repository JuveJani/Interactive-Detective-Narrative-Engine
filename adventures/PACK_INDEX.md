# IDNE Offline Adventure Pack Index

Spoiler-free overview of the four integration-test adventures. Each adventure is a self-contained fair-play detective mystery for a single investigator, designed for approximately two hours of play.

---

## The Harbor Light Signal

A Harbor Commission safety inspector arrives at a remote lighthouse station to verify a keeper's reported voluntary departure. Log books, weather records, supply manifests, and radio traffic tell conflicting stories about what happened overnight.

- **Role:** Harbor Commission safety inspector
- **Playtime:** ~120 minutes (59 player units)
- **Gamebook:** [`The_Harbor_Light_Signal/adventure/PLAYER/GAMEBOOK.md`](The_Harbor_Light_Signal/adventure/PLAYER/GAMEBOOK.md)

---

## The Gallery Verdict

An independent insurance adjuster investigates a missing featured painting after a private gallery opening. Conflicting witness accounts, security logs, and sightline evidence must be reconciled before the carrier claim deadline.

- **Role:** Independent insurance adjuster
- **Playtime:** ~120 minutes (61 player units)
- **Gamebook:** [`The_Gallery_Verdict/adventure/PLAYER/GAMEBOOK.md`](The_Gallery_Verdict/adventure/PLAYER/GAMEBOOK.md)

---

## The Quarry Silence

A county occupational safety investigator examines a night-shift fatality at a limestone quarry. Physical traces, equipment condition, and the official incident report do not align.

- **Role:** County occupational safety investigator
- **Playtime:** ~120 minutes (59 player units)
- **Gamebook:** [`The_Quarry_Silence/adventure/PLAYER/GAMEBOOK.md`](The_Quarry_Silence/adventure/PLAYER/GAMEBOOK.md)

---

## The Parish Ledger

A diocesan finance auditor reconciles memorial fund records at a small-town parish before the annual review. Donation ledgers, bank deposit batches, and parish council meeting minutes reveal discrepancies that someone tried to hide.

- **Role:** Diocesan finance auditor (parish council-appointed reviewer)
- **Playtime:** ~120 minutes (59 player units)
- **Gamebook:** [`The_Parish_Ledger/adventure/PLAYER/GAMEBOOK.md`](The_Parish_Ledger/adventure/PLAYER/GAMEBOOK.md)
