# DM Feeling — Human Playtest Questionnaire (Tier C)

Use after a full play session. Quote PLAYER text where requested.

## Agency
1. Did choices feel like understandable in-world actions? (1–5)
2. Any moment with no basis for deciding? Quote passage.

## Discovery
3. Did major information feel discovered rather than delivered? (1–5)
4. List clues that appeared without player effort.

## Exploration
5. Did locations and objects reward inspection? (1–5)
6. Note shallow rooms or objects fully exposed on arrival.

## Inference
7. Could you state your theory before the ending? (1–5)
8. Any checkbox or obvious inference?

## Aha
9. One moment information connected unexpectedly — or state none.

## World response
10. Did time, NPCs, or objects visibly change play? (1–5)

## Time pressure
11. Did the deadline affect decisions? (1–5)

## Failure
12. When checks failed, did something meaningful change?

## Conversation
13. Did NPC dialogue respond to your approach? (1–5)

## Ending
14. Did the ending follow from investigation? (1–5)

## Mode
15. Solo: partner/split remnants? Two-player: joint investigation share? (per player 1–5)

## Overall
16. Investigating a world vs reading a storybook? (1–5)
17. Confusion stalls and longest idle wait (minutes).
