# How to Play — The Cold Storage Alarm

**Mode:** Single investigator (you play the on-call technician).

**Playable file:** Use the static gamebook file in this folder as your only navigation source during play. It contains every section, numbered for turn-to navigation. The other thematic files are reference material; you do not need to look up internal unit codes.

**Starting section:** **636** (loading dock base menu). Read the opening at the start of the gamebook file, then turn to section **636**.

**Time:** Each action shows a time cost in minutes. The in-world clock moves forward. You cannot travel back to an earlier time.

**Checks:** Some actions require a d20 roll plus your character modifier. Each check allows **one attempt**. When a section tells you to roll, follow the separate **success** or **failure** turn-to instructions—never reuse the same section for both outcomes.

**Notes:** Record facts you learn in your case file. Synthesis steps ask you to connect records you have already found—answers are not delivered automatically. When a choice lists a section number, turn to that section only.

**Deadline:** At **5:00 a.m.**, compliance procedures may end your investigation window.

**Before you start:** read your character sheet for your check modifiers, and keep your case file open beside you to log clues as you find them.
