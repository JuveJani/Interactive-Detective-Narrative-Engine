# Player Test Guide — The Cold Storage Alarm (Spoiler-Free)

**Package status:** Pre-Playtest  
**Mode:** Single investigator  
**Format:** Static book (read sections, choose actions, record notes)

This guide tells you how to run a playtest session. It does not reveal solutions, suspects, or ending outcomes.

---

## What you need

- The PLAYER materials inside the adventure package (or printed copies).
- Your **character sheet** (check modifiers for d20 rolls).
- A **case file** or notebook to log facts as you find them.
- A clock or timer for wall-clock measurement (see the playtime recording sheet).
- Pen or pencil.

---

## Before you start

1. Read **How to Play** in the PLAYER folder.
2. Read your **character sheet** for skill modifiers.
3. Open a blank case file page for tonight's notes.
4. Note your **session start time** on the playtime recording sheet.
5. Do not read ahead into locations, objects, or endings you have not reached in play.

---

## How a session works

1. **Opening** — Read the opening scene. It sets your role, the alarm, and the compliance deadline.
2. **Locations** — At each place, read the base section for that location. It lists actions you can take.
3. **Choices** — Pick one action. Actions use plain language (go somewhere, examine something, speak to someone).
4. **Time** — Actions advance an in-world clock. You cannot return to an earlier time.
5. **Checks** — Some actions need a d20 roll plus your modifier. Each check allows **one attempt**. Read the result text for success or failure separately.
6. **Notes** — Write down names, times, and facts you discover. Synthesis steps ask you to connect records you already found.
7. **Deadline** — At **5:00 a.m.** in-world, compliance procedures may end your investigation window.
8. **Ending** — When you reach an ending section, read it and stop. Record which ending you reached.

---

## What to pay attention to (for feedback)

You do not need to solve a checklist. These questions help the designers improve the adventure:

- Were choices understandable as in-world actions?
- Did you ever want to do something logical that was not offered?
- Did reading feel too slow, too fast, or about right?
- Did you need to reread sections? How long?
- Did the deadline affect your decisions?
- Was there a moment when separate facts suddenly connected?
- Did any section feel too short or too long?

Record answers on the **Actual Playtime Recording Sheet** after the session.

---

## What not to do during this playtest

- Do not look up author-only or logic packages (`DO_NOT_READ` folders).
- Do not use a simulator or automated solver unless you are explicitly asked to run diagnostics.
- Do not share ending text or solution details publicly before the playtest window closes.

---

## Suggested pacing

The package includes a **predicted** playtime of roughly two to three hours for a thorough investigation path. That number is an unverified model estimate — your actual time may be shorter or longer. Measure wall-clock time honestly; breaks do not count toward play time.

---

## After the session

1. Record **end time** and total wall-clock minutes (breaks excluded).
2. Complete every field on `ACTUAL_PLAYTIME_RECORDING_SHEET.md`.
3. Optionally complete the Tier C questionnaire in `adventure/DO_NOT_READ/dm_feeling_reports/tier_c_playtest_questionnaire.md` (designer-facing; may contain reflection prompts that reference your session).

---

## Support

If a section reference seems broken or a choice leads nowhere, note the location and section name on your recording sheet. Do not guess hidden content — report the stall.

**Thank you for playtesting.**
