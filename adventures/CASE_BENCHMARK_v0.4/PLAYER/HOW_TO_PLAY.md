# How to Play

## Core loop

1. Read the scene together (Joint) or alone (Split).
2. Choose an **investigative action** — never a page code.
3. Pay the time cost on the case file.
4. Record clues and tags when told.
5. At splits, reunite only at the regroup scene named.

## Time

- One shared clock. It only moves forward.
- After a split, advance by the **longer** role's time, plus any regroup cost stated.
- Phone/text while split costs **+10 minutes** per exchange.

## Checks

When a scene sends you to a check destination:

1. Roll d20 + your focus bonus (character sheet).
2. Read the **destination** for pass or fail.
3. The decision scene never told you the outcome.

## Clues

Log clues on the case file as **C-01** through **C-14**.  
Infer worksheets are **I-01** through **I-03**.

## Hubs

Hub scenes list actions only. The next scene holds what you find.

## Follow-up slots

The case file has two "Follow up on: ___" lines. Write a person, place, or record.  
The next hub tells you if something new appears (max 2 uses).

## Win condition

Build method, motive, and opportunity proof on your sheet.  
Complete infer worksheets. Accuse only when you can justify it.

## Do not

- Share private booklet text during splits.
- Open endings early.
- Use honor-system guessing — endings follow your sheet.
