## J-100 — Arrival

Saturday, 19:00. Harborview Arcade, a brick mixed-use building: bakery on the ground floor, apartments above, manager office off the lobby, rear service stairs to the basement.

County dispatch sent you as a pair: one of you handles people, one handles records. Police tape blocks the rear stairwell. A woman — Elena Voss, bookkeeper and tenant — died from a head injury at the stair bottom. First responders treated it as a possible fall.

**Your institutional stake:** A grant-funded facade program requires your preliminary report by **23:00**. Incomplete or unfair work has real consequences for your office.

Officer Park meets you in the lobby. Four tenants and staff are available for questions. The manager can open records. The basement storage room holds association files.

**Building map (orientation only — not a clue):**

- Lobby, manager office, bakery, rear stairwell, basement.

Set clock to **19:00**.

**Continue to J-120.**
