# Setup

## 1. Assign roles

| Role | Booklet | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| **People Investigator** | `BOOKLET_PEOPLE.md` | Interviews, witnesses, rapport |
| **Records Investigator** | `BOOKLET_RECORDS.md` | Documents, logs, physical evidence |

Both players are county building-safety investigators. Choose your own names.

## 2. Stakes (read aloud)

**People:** Your last case drew a formal complaint. A clean, fair resolution restores your credibility.

**Records:** Grant funding for your unit is up for renewal. A sloppy report could cost the whole team.

## 3. Prepare materials

- Print or open three views: shared joint file, People booklet, Records booklet.
- Seal `ENDINGS.md` until **J-600**.
- Each player takes the matching character sheet.

## 4. Clock

- Start at **19:00** on the case file.
- Preliminary report deadline: **23:00**.
- Thresholds: **20:00**, **21:00**, **22:00** — mark each when reached.

## 5. Begin

Open `JOINT_SCENES.md` at **J-100**.
